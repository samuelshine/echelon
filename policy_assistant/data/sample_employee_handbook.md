# Northstar Labs Employee Handbook

Effective date: January 1, 2026. Policy owner: People Operations.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. New employees accrue time monthly from their start date. Up to five unused days carry into the next calendar year; any balance above five days expires on December 31 unless local law requires otherwise.

Employees should request planned leave in the HR portal at least ten business days in advance. The employee's manager approves the request after considering team coverage. Unexpected illness does not require advance notice, but the employee should notify their manager as soon as practical.

## Remote and hybrid work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role is designated hybrid. Each team publishes two core collaboration days when hybrid employees are expected to work from their assigned office. A manager may approve a temporary exception for health, caregiving, travel disruption, or another documented business reason.

Working outside the employee's country of employment requires written approval from People Operations, Tax, and Information Security before travel is booked. Approval is not guaranteed and may be limited to 20 working days in a calendar year.

## Information security

Company confidential information must be stored only in approved systems. Employees must not paste customer data, source code, credentials, or non-public financial information into personal accounts or unapproved AI tools.

A lost device, suspected phishing message, exposed credential, or accidental disclosure must be reported to the Security team through the incident portal immediately. Employees should not delay a report while attempting to investigate the incident themselves.

## Expenses and travel

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursable when they have a documented business purpose and an itemized receipt. Manager pre-approval is required for any single expense above $500. Air travel should be booked in economy class; premium economy is allowed for a scheduled flight longer than eight hours with manager approval.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. Finance may reject late reports unless the employee provides a documented reason for the delay.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose outside employment, board service, significant financial interests in a supplier or competitor, and close personal relationships that could affect a workplace decision. Disclosures should be sent to the Ethics team before the activity begins. Disclosure does not automatically prohibit the activity; Ethics will document any safeguards or restrictions.

## Respectful workplace

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. Anyone who experiences or witnesses concerning conduct may report it to their manager, People Operations, the Ethics hotline, or the anonymous reporting portal. Managers who receive a report must forward it to People Operations and must not conduct their own investigation.
